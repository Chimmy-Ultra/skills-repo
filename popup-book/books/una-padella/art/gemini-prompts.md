# Una Padella：Gemini 生圖 prompt

一共 3 張。每張開一個新對話，貼上整段 prompt。生好的圖用下面的檔名存，三張一起傳回來。

---

## 1. cover.png（直式 2:3）

```
A vertical book cover illustration, portrait 2:3. An antique hand-coloured engraved map of the Italian peninsula with Sicily and Sardinia, 18th-century cartography style: fine copperplate hatching for coastlines and mountains, pale washes of faded green, ochre and rose on the land, a plain sea with a few delicate wave lines, a small compass rose in the lower right, a thin double-ruled border around the whole page. The map fills only the lower two thirds of the page; the upper third is empty aged cream paper with nothing on it, reserved for a title. Quiet, sparse, lots of empty paper, old-book texture. Absolutely no text, no letters, no place names, no cartouche, no labels anywhere.
```

## 2. sheet-a.png（橫式 3:2）

```
Six separate objects arranged in a 3x2 grid on a pure white background, each object isolated with generous white space around it, none touching or overlapping, none touching the edges. Style: 19th-century hand-coloured copperplate engraving, fine line hatching, soft muted watercolour tints, like a plate from an old household encyclopedia. Even neutral light, no cast shadows, no ground, no background.
Top row: (1) a small single-burner portable induction cooktop with a flat black glass top and a small control panel, seen at a three-quarter angle from above; (2) an empty round non-stick frying pan about 26 cm across with a long handle, three-quarter view from above; (3) a bundle of dry spaghetti tied loosely, lying diagonally.
Bottom row: (4) a small paper sachet of instant soup powder, plain cream-coloured paper with no printing; (5) a block of butter on its opened paper wrapper; (6) a few thin slices of raw beef, rolled hot-pot style, on a small square of butcher paper.
Absolutely no text, letters, labels or logos on any object.
```

```
Six separate small kitchen vignettes arranged in a 3x2 grid on a pure white background, each isolated with generous white space around it, none touching or overlapping, none touching the edges. Every vignette shows the same round frying pan with a long handle, about 26 cm across, seen at a three-quarter angle from above. Style: 19th-century hand-coloured copperplate engraving, fine line hatching, soft muted watercolour tints, like a plate from an old cookery book. Even neutral light, no cast shadows, no ground, no table, no stove, no background.
Top row: (1) the pan full of boiling water with a bundle of dry spaghetti standing in it, the long dry ends sticking out over the rim of the pan; (2) the same pan with the spaghetti now softened and bent, fully under the water, a pair of wooden chopsticks resting across the pan; (3) the pan tilted to one side, cloudy water pouring out over the rim, the spaghetti held back inside the pan by a pair of chopsticks.
Bottom row: (4) the pan with cooked spaghetti tossed in a smooth pale creamy sauce, a small piece of butter melting on top; (5) the pan with a thin layer of creamy sauce and a little simmering liquid at the bottom, thin slices of beef laid flat in it, some still pink, some cooked brown; (6) the empty pan being wiped clean with a folded white paper towel held by a hand.
```
